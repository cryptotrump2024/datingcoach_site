## PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Months 1-2) | Budget: $8,000-$14,000/month

### 1.1 TikTok Launch Strategy

**Why TikTok First:** TikTok delivers the highest influencer ROI (75% of advertisers report best returns) with the lowest CPI ($0.50-$2.50 vs $2-$5.50 on Meta). Users spend 58.4 minutes daily on the platform, and 54% engage with brand content daily.

**Content Strategy - "Treat TikTok Like a TV Show":**

| Content Series | Format | Frequency | Example |
|---------------|--------|-----------|---------|
| "Worst Text Ever" | 15-30s video | 2x/week | Show cringeworthy text + VibeChat improved version |
| "Before/After Rizz" | 15-30s split-screen | 2x/week | Same scenario, bad response vs. AI-trained response |
| "She Said What?!" | 20-30s reaction | 1x/week | Decode hidden meaning of real messages |
| "3 Texts That Kill" | 15s quick-tip | 1x/week | Hook-driven educational |

**Hook Templates (First 3 Seconds - 80% of success):**
- "You've been texting her all wrong..."
- "Wait, this doesn't make sense..."
- "Why does she always stop replying?"
- "I was getting ghosted until I tried this"
- "The reason she ghosted you has nothing to do with your looks"

**TikTok SEO:**
- Target keywords: "how to text a girl," "what to say on dating apps," "dating advice for guys," "rizz tips," "conversation starters"
- Hashtag strategy: 1 broad (#DatingAdvice) + 2 medium (#TextingTips #Rizz) + 2 niche (#DatingSimulator #VibeChat)
- Post Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-12pm and 7pm-11pm

**Budget Allocation:**
- Organic content creation: $1,500-$2,000
- Spark Ads (paid amplification): $1,500-$2,500
- Nano/micro influencer seeding: $1,500-$2,500
- Content production (UGC-style): $500-$1,000

### 1.2 Instagram Foundation

**Strategy:** Carousels are the highest-engagement format (1.92% avg), Reels get 2-3x algorithmic reach. Focus on educational carousels + entertaining Reels.

**Content Pillars:**
1. **Educational Carousels** (3x/week): "5 Texts That Make Her Lose Interest," "The Psychology of Attraction in Texts"
2. **Reels** (3x/week): Before/after transformations, app demos, "POV: Your rizz after VibeChat"
3. **Stories** (daily): Polls "Which reply wins? A or B," Q&As, behind-the-scenes
4. **UGC Reposts** (2x/week): User transformation stories

**Budget:** $2,000-$3,000/month (60% Reels ads, 20% influencer partnerships, 20% content creation)

### 1.3 Influencer Seeding Campaign

**Phase 1: Seeding (Month 1-2)**
- Partner with 20-50 **nano influencers** (1K-10K followers) in dating/self-improvement
- Gift Pro access + $100-$500 per post
- Content types: honest reviews, "day in the life" texting experiments, before/after
- **Total cost:** $5,000-$10,000
- **Goal:** Generate 50-100 authentic UGC videos

**Target Influencer Types:**
1. Dating/Relationship Coaches (micro-mid tier)
2. Men's Self-Improvement Creators
3. Lifestyle/Dating Comedy Creators
4. "Rizz" Content Creators
5. Fitness/Masculinity Creators

**Influencer Pricing (2025-2026):**

| Tier | Followers | TikTok Cost | Instagram Cost |
|------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| Nano | 1K-10K | $5-$300 | $25-$300 |
| Micro | 10K-100K | $200-$1,200 | $250-$3,000 |
| Mid-tier | 100K-500K | $1,200-$5,000 | $1,600-$15,000 |

## PRICING STRATEGY & UNIT ECONOMICS

### Three-Tier Pricing (Optimized)

| Tier | Monthly | Annual (Save 40%) | What's Included |
|------|---------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **FREE** | $0 | - | 3 conversations, 1 profile analysis, basic feedback, limited personas |
| **PRO** | $9.95/mo | $59.99/yr ($5/mo) | 50 conversations/mo, full analysis, all personas, save history, dashboard |
| **ADVANCED** | $29.95/mo | $179.99/yr ($15/mo) | Unlimited everything, custom personas, relationship coaching, priority support |

**Why This Works:**
- Three-tier pricing increases hero-tier conversions 15-20% via decoy effect
- $9.95 uses charm pricing (left-digit bias: perceived as ~$9 not $10)
- Annual plans monetize ~2x better than monthly
- Crypto payments accepted: **10% discount** for USDT/USDC/BTC (privacy appeal for dating apps)
